# Information this Site collects

This Site does not actively ask you to provide personal identifying information. You can browse all articles anonymously. During your visit, the following data may be processed technically:

- **Server logs**: this Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record standard access information (such as IP address, browser type, and visit time) under its own policy. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Pageview statistics**: this Site may display aggregate figures such as anonymous visitor counts and pageviews, which are not used to identify individuals.
- **Local storage**: this Site may use the browser's `localStorage` to remember your preferences (such as dark mode or language choice). This data is stored only on your device and is not sent to this Site or any third party.

This Site may use or embed third-party services, which may set their own cookies:

- **Google AdSense (if enabled)**: this Site may display ads via Google AdSense in the future. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this Site or other sites.
    - Google's explanation of its use of cookies for advertising, and how to disable personalized ads: [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/), [Google Advertising Technologies & Privacy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
    - You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies via [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) or [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/).
- **Other embedded content**: articles may embed external images or links (such as imgur, YouTube, GitHub), and these services have their own privacy policies.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, though some features may be affected as a result.
