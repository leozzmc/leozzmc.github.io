## Intro

Having grown up in Taipei for years without visiting Qixing Mountain's main-peak triangulation marker, I took advantage of a rare day of good weather to go check it out.

The main route for this Qixing Mountain hike was from the **Xiaoyoukeng trailhead** -> **Qixing Mountain main peak** -> **Qixing Mountain east peak**, then back the same way to the **Xiaoyoukeng parking lot**.

![](/img/mountain-1/161346.jpg)

Qixing Mountain is the highest mountain within Taipei City, reaching an elevation of `1120` meters.

## Xiaoyoukeng geothermal vent

The sulfur smell near the geothermal vent is strong, but on a cold day it's actually pretty warm close up.

## Qixing Mountain main peak

The path to the main peak is almost all stone steps, but there are quite a few steep climbs along the way, so **I recommend bringing trekking poles**.

![](/img/mountain-1/m13.jpg)
![](/img/mountain-1/m8.jpg)

It's silvergrass season in November, but it's windy here and the silvergrass is all bent over by the wind, though the scenery is still pretty.

![](/img/mountain-1/m7.jpg)
![](/img/mountain-1/m10.jpg)

On weekends there are many hikers who come to take photos with this marker post, so decide for yourself whether to grab a memento.
![](/img/mountain-1/m9.jpg)

## Qixing Mountain east peak

From the main peak to the east peak is only another `0.3K`, so you can collect both summits in one go. But some sections from the main peak to the east peak are slippery, so I recommend wearing anti-slip hiking shoes.

## The bushes

By the way, all along the trail there are many passable bush tunnels like the one below. If you check an offline map you can see there's a path, and you can also find tied ropes. An old friend went in for a bit of exploring, but there was too much mud further on, so we didn't go deeper.

![](/img/mountain-1/m11.jpg)
![](/img/mountain-1/m12.jpg)
![](/img/mountain-1/m14.jpg)

Personally I thought the east peak's scenery was so-so, but plenty of people still line up for photos.

![](/img/mountain-1/m15.jpg)

Then I went back the same way to the parking lot, ending today's trip.

> Actually, most people go up from Xiaoyoukeng to the main peak then the east peak, then down via Lengshuikeng, or the reverse route. But since I drove, I could only go back and forth the same way.
