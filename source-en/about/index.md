# About this blog

Hi, I'm **Kevin Liu (leozzmc)**, and this is where I record my technical learning and hands-on work.

The content here roughly covers:

- **Security / CTF**: CTF writeups (GaianSpace, STARPWN, etc.), penetration-testing and defensive notes
- **Radio / Space**: LoRa, Meshtastic, SDR, satellite communications, and RF signal analysis
- **Cloud / DevOps**: hands-on AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD, and serverless
- **Algorithms / Problem solving**: data-structure notes and LeetCode solutions
- **Paper reading**, and the occasional **hiking** log

I previously worked as a Cloud Support Engineer at AWS. I enjoy building things, reverse engineering, and turning what I learn into notes. Nearly all the articles on this site are my own first-hand writeups and hands-on records, and I hope they're useful to people with similar interests.
